## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor, mandatory, owned by methodology): From an editorial standpoint, this dimension asks whether the way the work was designed, how its data or artifacts were handled, how results are reported statistically, and what it offers others to reproduce it would survive scrutiny by a competent referee in this field. It is not mine to score, but a failure here would by itself force a major revision or a rejection, so I will treat the methodology seat's verdict as a gate on any decision I recommend.

D2 (domain_accuracy, mandatory, owned by domain): This dimension asks whether what the manuscript asserts matches the current state of evidence in its field, whether it describes earlier work fairly and correctly, and whether its technical vocabulary and reported results contain no factual mistakes. As editor I depend on the domain seat for this judgment, and an error found here weighs directly on whether the contribution can be published as stated.

D3 (argumentative_coherence, mandatory, owned by the devil's advocate, also open to methodology): This dimension asks whether the central thesis holds together without contradicting itself, whether the evidence offered actually carries the weight of the claims made, and whether any reasoning fallacy undermines the main line of argument. Editorially, a paper whose claims outrun its support cannot be accepted regardless of how well it reads or how well it fits the venue.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance, high, owned by perspective): This dimension asks whether readers from neighbouring fields can follow the framing, definitions, and stated implications, and whether any claim that reaches across disciplines is backed by evidence. A block here would trigger a major revision on its own, so I will read the perspective seat's findings as a signal of how broad the readership can be.

D5 (writing_and_structure, normal, owned by me as eic): This dimension asks whether the manuscript is organised so that a reader can find and follow its argument, whether the exposition is clear, whether its figures and tables are legible and earn their place, and whether it follows the formatting and reporting conventions a reader in this venue would expect. A shortfall here moves the decision to minor revision at most unless it is severe enough to stop review.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution, mandatory, owned by me as eic): This dimension asks whether the manuscript belongs at the configured venue and whether it adds something original and significant that the venue's readers need. Because it is mandatory, a fatal finding here leads to rejection and a block leads to major revision, so this is the dimension where my editorial judgment carries the most weight.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A clear statement of the problem and contribution early on, a section order that matches the logical flow of the argument, terms defined before use, figures and tables that are readable and referenced in the text, length proportionate to content, and adherence to the conventions expected of a submission in the stated field.
what_triggers_block: Organisation or exposition so poor that a referee cannot reliably locate or reconstruct the main claims and their supporting evidence, or figures and tables that are missing, unreadable, or contradict the text they support, such that substantive review cannot proceed until the manuscript is restructured.
what_triggers_warn: The argument is recoverable but the reader is slowed by problems such as redundant or overlong sections, undefined or inconsistent terminology, figures or tables that need clearer labels or captions, or departures from expected reporting conventions that a focused revision can fix.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: An explicit and accurate positioning of the contribution against prior work, a contribution that is new rather than a restatement of known results, significance that a reader of the configured venue would recognise, and a scope and framing that suit that venue's readership.
what_triggers_block: The contribution is real but its novelty or significance is not established in the manuscript as written, for example because positioning against closely related work is missing or because the main claims are framed more broadly than the delivered results support, requiring major reworking before the venue can judge it.
what_triggers_warn: The contribution is original and in scope, but its significance or delineation from prior work is understated, partly unclear, or framed in a way that will limit uptake by the venue's readers, fixable through targeted revision of framing and related-work discussion.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript falls outside the configured venue's scope, or it offers no original contribution because its central results are already known or are a minor restatement of existing work, so that no revision could make it publishable at this venue.
